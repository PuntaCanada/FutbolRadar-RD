# Sponsorship review — not approved for publication

Advertising, affiliate links and placements remain disabled. This is a compliance checklist, not a determination that gambling advertising is lawful.

- Determine actual operator, target jurisdictions and each advertiser's current licence.
- Obtain written legal review of applicable advertising restrictions, remuneration and cross-border access.
- Do not advertise to minors; keep academy, youth and women's section (which contains youth material) free of gambling promotion.
- No tracking, external advertising scripts, affiliate IDs, betting links, odds or bonuses are enabled.
- Review privacy, clear advertising labels, responsible gambling messaging, age protection and advertiser contract before any separate implementation.
- No automatic activation upon updating this document or configuration.
